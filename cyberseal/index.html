      Already have an account?

      Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (310,000 rounds) and never stored or sent anywhere. There's no password reset, so keep yours safe.

      Delete account?

      This permanently removes your account and its signing key from this device. Documents you already sealed still verify, but you can never seal with this key again.
